How do I…

Things the editor does that you can't see by looking at it. On a Windows keyboard, ⌘ is Ctrl.

…select cells (a column, a block, several cells)?
  • Select one cell: click it. It gets a double outline and becomes the starting cell: its value is the one that gets copied to the others.
  • Select a block: click the starting cell, then Shift+click the opposite corner. Everything between is selected.
  • Select a whole column: ⌘+click (Mac) / Ctrl+click its heading (a plain click never selects a column; while Sort or Filter is on, a plain click still sorts or filters).Several columns: ⌘/Ctrl+click more headings to add or remove them, or Shift+click a heading to select every column between. Move them: drag any selected heading and all selected columns move together, keeping their order. The top cell of the first column you clicked is the starting cell (the value that gets copied). The selection only covers books and columns currently shown, so use a filter first to limit the books.
  • Select scattered cells: ⌘+click (Mac) or Ctrl+click adds a cell or removes it if it is already selected. ⌘+clicking the starting cell clears the selection.
  • Clear the selection: Esc, the Clear button in the bar at the bottom, or a plain click on any cell.
  • Right-click a cell that isn't selected and it is selected first, then the menu opens.
…copy one value into many cells?
  • Select the starting cell (the value to copy), then Shift+click or ⌘+click the cells to fill. A bar appears at the bottom: “Copy to N selected cells: …”. Press the ⧉ button in it.
  • You are asked to confirm. The fill saves straight to Supabase, one cell at a time. Undo (⌘+Z) reverses the whole fill in one step.
  • Only editable cells are filled. A cell is skipped if the value doesn't fit its column (text into a Yes/No column, a decimal into a whole-number column, a date it can't read, a value that isn't on a pick-one list). Yes/No accepts yes, no, true, false or blank; numbers ignore $, commas and spaces.
  • If Supabase refuses one cell, the fill stops there. The message says how many were filled; Undo reverses those.
  • To copy a cell's text to the clipboard instead, hover the cell and press the small ⧉ at its top right.
…edit a cell from the keyboard?
  • A plain click only selects. To edit: Enter or F2 on the selected cell, or right-click → Edit (on a read-only column it says “not editable”; click it, or press Enter / F2 on the cell, to see why and how to change it instead).
  • ↑ ↓ ← → move to the next editable cell (read-only cells are skipped).
  • In the editor: Enter saves and moves down to the next editable cell; Shift+Enter adds a new line in long text; Tab saves and moves right; Shift+Tab saves and moves left; Esc cancels; clicking outside the editor saves.
  • Undo ⌘+Z; Redo ⌘+Shift+Z (or Ctrl+Y). They don't work while you are typing in a box; use the Undo / Redo buttons at the top then.
  • Right-click a cell in a Formats column with no format yet → “Add … for this book”; right-click a story bible cell with none → “Add a story bible for this book”.
  • Right-click a column heading (or its name in the column list) → Rename column…. Choose Nickname to show a shorter name (right-click → Show full name shows the original), or Change the name permanently. Back to the original name undoes either. Everyone sees the new name, on any device. Many columns start with a built-in short nickname (BN Bk#, Pb $, U-link, Store #…); rename them the same way.
  • Right-click a cell that holds a web address (store links, Website book post, affiliate links…) → Open link in new tab.
  • Right-click a cell in a column you edit in Notion (Reviews, Marketing copy, and the story lists such as Main characters) → Open this book's Notion page. It opens at the heading of that column's section (“🔄 Shared with Supabase” or Story details). A page not synced since this was added opens at the top until its next sync.
…remove a tag from Tags and Display tags?
  • Tags is every tag the story has. Display tags is the smaller set of those that is shown on Payhip to tell S.E. Smith books apart. They are not duplicates, and the editions of a story (EN, FR, DE, IT) share both.
  • Every display tag must also be in Tags. So remove the tag from Display tags first and save, then remove it from Tags and save. Doing Tags first is refused with “Display tags must also be in Tags: …”.
  • To add a tag, do it the other way round: add it to Tags first, then to Display tags. A tag that isn't in Tag Vocab yet asks if you want to add it.
…change a Long Synopsis, its order, or a promo line?
  • Right-click a prose cell (Reasons to Love, loglines, a synopsis or series synopsis) and the top line of the menu says Written by Ai or Written by a human. It is a rule of thumb: Reasons to Love is Ai; loglines are by a human when the book came out more than two years ago, Ai when newer; series synopses are by a human; other synopses are by a human unless three or more paragraphs are a single short sentence each. If it is wrong, choose Change to … under it; that is saved in Supabase (settings → editor_prose_authors) and wins over the rule, and Go back to the rule of thumb undoes it. A series synopsis change applies to the whole series.
  • Right-click a Long Synopsis (built) cell → Edit. A popup shows its parts side by side, in the order the Long Synopsis uses: award line, series intro, essential synopsis, promo line. Type in the boxes and pick the promo line; the preview at the bottom shows the result. Save changes this book (and the promo line on the books you chose), and Undo reverses it in one step. The Essential Description is also a column of its own.
  • The ◀ ▶ arrows, Show in every Long Synopsis and Next part starts (in the same paragraph or in a new paragraph) set the order and spacing for every book. You are asked before it is saved, and Undo does not reverse it: change it back in the popup.
  • “In the same paragraph” still starts a new paragraph when the next text is a story title (a line with no end punctuation). The essential synopsis is always shown.
  • The promo line goes to the books chosen under Apply this promo line to in the popup: Just this book, All books that use this book's current promo line, or All books in this language. The same choice is on right-click a Long Synopsis (built) cell → Change promo line…. Undo reverses all of them in one step.
…find books and keep several on screen?
  • Under the Find a book box, tick Add the previous book in the series and/or Add the corresponding English book to also show those books next to every book shown (even if the language, series or search would hide them). They are not saved in views.
  • The Find a book box looks in the title and file name only. Tick Expand scope to search every ticked column too. With several words, a book must contain all of them.
  • Press Enter (or Add to list) to keep the books found, then type the next search. Kept books stay at the top; what you are typing now is listed after them. Clear list empties it; right-click a kept book → “Remove from list” removes just that one.
  • Right-click a row → Hide row or Hide from here to end. They stay hidden until Show hidden rows (in the Books section or the right-click menu).
  • The Language and Series drop-downs narrow the list too; “Send shown to Notion”, Export → “Only what I see” and “Find store links for shown books” all use whatever is shown after these.
…choose, hide, move and freeze columns?
  • Tick columns in the sidebar. Type in “Find a column…” and press Check all shown to tick every column the search finds. Right-click a section heading → Check all / Uncheck all. Right-click a column name → “What's this?” (when it has an explanation) and “Does this go to Notion?”. In that dialog's list, right-click an item → Don't go to Notion.
  • Right-click a column heading → Does this go to Notion?, Clear filter, Hide (Hide column, Hide from here to end), Freeze up to this column (it and everything to its left stay put when you scroll sideways), Unfreeze, Reset (this column's width, all column widths, column order). Title, language, series and number are always shown (the Blanks view keeps only the title).
  • Drag a heading to move the column (a blue edge shows where it lands). Drag the right edge of a heading to resize; double-click that edge to reset the width.
  • Show full text (above the grid) shows long cells in full; otherwise only the first line shows.
  • Blanks (Columns section) shows the title plus every column that has a blank to fill for at least one book shown. Striped cells are empty because the book has no such thing (no award, no subtitle, no such format) and don't count. It loads every column, so it can take a minute.
…sort and filter?
  • Selected a column first? ⌘/Ctrl+click its heading to select it, then press Sort (sorts it A→Z; press Sort again for Z→A) or Filter (opens its filter box). With several columns selected, Sort stacks them left to right and Filter opens the leftmost one.
  • Sort button, then click headings: first click A→Z, second Z→A, third removes it. Sorts stack like Excel: the heading you click last is the main sort, and the earlier ones stay inside it (click title, then heat, and the books are grouped by heat, with titles in order inside each heat). Clear sort removes all. Press Esc to leave Sort / Filter mode without choosing.
  • Filter button then a heading. The list shows each value with how many books have it (counted after your other filters). Typing in “Search values” ticks what it finds, like Excel. Enter in the search or condition boxes = OK.
  • Condition: Contains, Doesn't contain, Equals, Doesn't equal, Greater than, Less than, Between, Is blank, Is not blank (dates say After / Before). It combines with the ticked values.
  • Clear all filters appears above the grid once any filter is on.
…save a view and open a book from a link?
  • Save view (Columns section) stores the ticked columns, sort, filters, column order, widths and frozen columns in Supabase, so it follows you to any device. Update view overwrites the selected view with what you have now. Older views from Excel keep columns only and can't be updated here.
  • A link ending in #book=<file name>&col=<column id> opens the editor on that book (and flashes that cell). The Distribution Clipboard Queue app makes these. The link view shows only that book, its title and the linked column; press Show all books & columns to go back.
  • Export downloads a CSV (opens in Excel): “Only what I see” or “Everything”.
…see where each new book is in its release?
  • Press Release at the top, then choose Closest release to today, Most recent activity (most edits logged in the last 30 days) or Both; only those book(s) load, so it doesn't time out. Each book not released yet (or released in the last 60 days) shows its steps in order. English: Before Subsc Pub → Payhip → Wide release. Other languages: Before release → Release and after (Payhip is part of the release).
  • ✓ steps are done; a red ! step is late. Get ISBNs is due by Subsc Pub (English) or the release date (other languages). Get store links is due 2 days after that format's date (eBook and paperback from the Pub Date, hardcover and audiobook from their own dates) and stays open until every link is in.
  • Steps tick themselves from the catalog: Book details (the script check and your inputs), ISBNs, page count, Payhip product Live, story bible approved, Vellum link created, store links. Uploaded to the stores is ticked by the Distribution app when it finishes a distributor (or when all the eBook and paperback links are in).
  • Formatted in Vellum, Cover done and Added to the subscription post have a box to tick (Cover also ticks itself when the cover image link is filled). They count as done once their date has passed. Unticking removes your tick.
  • Ticks are kept in Supabase (table release_steps) and logged in the change log.
…send books to Notion, and what exactly happens?

Sending

  • Send shown to Notion sends the books shown in the grid after your filters (one click), with all the Notion columns for each, not only the columns you can see.
  • Each book is added to the queue unless it is already Waiting or Working. Then the sync starts. If it can't start right away, the every-minute schedule starts it.
  • The top bar shows “N waiting for Notion · N failed”. Click the red “failed” to see the reason for each book and press “Try these again”, or “Dismiss” to clear a line without sending the book. You can close the page; the sync carries on.

How the sync runs (Supabase Edge Function notion-sync)

  • One book at a time, about 10 seconds each (it pauses 0.34 seconds between Notion requests because Notion allows about 3 a second). A run works up to about 100 seconds, then the next run carries on; only one run works at a time.
  • Every minute a schedule starts a run if anything is Waiting. A request Notion turns down as too busy (or a temporary error) is retried up to 5 times.
  • Only owners and editors can start it from the editor; the schedule uses a secret kept in Supabase Vault. The Notion token is in Vault too (name notion_token).
  • A queue line goes Waiting → Working → Done (or Failed, with the reason). Done lines are deleted after 30 days.

What a book's Notion page looks like

  • 📚 From Supabase (a box at the top): every field marked as going to Notion (right-click any column → “Does this go to Notion?” lists them, and its Send to Notion / Stop sending to Notion button changes it). Blank fields (empty, n/a, n/d) are left out, and Translator and Proofreader show only for translations. A translation shows its English beside a field when the two differ. The box is rebuilt at every sync: edit these in the Catalog Editor. If someone changes a “Label: value” line inside the box in Notion, that field moves to the Shared section (“Other shared details”), its new value is saved here, and from then on it syncs both ways for that book. Any other line changed inside the box is kept in the Shared section under “Changed in Notion”.
  • 🔄 Shared with Supabase: Loglines, Excerpts, Teasers, Awards, Links (series page, SES Store = Payhip links, BookFunnel, store links, cover image and alt text, website book post and the other marketing links), Store IDs (Amazon ASINs and Apple ID), Reviews and every kind of marketing copy. It syncs both ways: a change made in Notion is saved here, a change made here shows there; additions from both sides are kept; when both sides changed the same link, Notion's wins. A new “Label: link” line adds a new marketing link; a new heading adds a new kind of marketing copy. Saves from Notion are logged in the change log as “Notion sync”, so they can be undone.
  • 📝 Notion only: never copied here. A duplicate always ends up once, in the Shared section: a field from the box typed or pasted into Notion only moves to the Shared section (“Other shared details”, two-way from then on), and anything that repeats the Shared section is removed from Notion only. Old-system sections (Loglines, Teasers, Awards, buy links, Website, BookFunnel, Book Cover, Reviews, “Archived Notion …”) are merged into the Shared section.
  • Clearing out the old system happens only once per page, at its first sync with this layout: lines with nothing after the “:”, empty list items, leftover dividers and the old template lines (“Title:”, “Release Date:”, “Keywords:”, “Digital Price:” …) are removed. After that, an empty line or a “Label:” someone types in Notion only is left alone.
  • Long Synopsis goes to Notion. It is put together in Supabase from Award line + Series intro + Essential synopsis + Promo line, and the sync sends the finished text (under its own heading in the box). Notion doesn't put it back together: change its parts here. If its text is changed in the box or pasted into Notion only, Long Synopsis moves to the Shared section (still showing the text Supabase builds) and the Notion edit is kept under “Changed in Notion”. Other worked-out columns (Series & book number, Book # with no decimals, Author, Publisher) work the same way.
  • Can't go to Notion (right-click the column → Does this go to Notion? says why): Min Age (send Rating), Promo line (it is inside Long Synopsis), affiliate links (worked out from the store links, which go), the English text kept on translations (shown beside each translated field automatically), Edition differences, Books2Read store count, award badge, Bestselling author line, Translation Cache, and the story lists Pet names & invented words, Pronunciations, Scene image prompts and Author's Note.
  • An edition with no Notion page gets a new one: English books in SES Books, other languages in SES Books → the language page (French, German…), named “English Title - Language”, placed under the book's series heading after the books with lower numbers (a new series gets its own heading, in alphabetical order). A page with that name that is already there is used instead. A page Notion says it can't find usually needs sharing with the integration: in Notion ••• → Connections.

What queues a book by itself

  • Changing anything the Notion box shows queues that book, and every other book it also appears on (other editions of the story, books that recommend a retitled book, other books with the same character, and so on). Only books whose Notion page has been synced at least once are queued; an edit never creates a Notion page; a book already Waiting isn't queued twice.
  • Reviews and marketing copy are not watched (they are edited in Notion); the nightly refresh carries them over.
  • Every night at 06:23 UTC (2:23 am New York in summer, 1:23 am in winter) every page the catalog created, and every page synced at least once, is queued again. This catches recommendation changes caused by other books.
